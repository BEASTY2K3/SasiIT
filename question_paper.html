SECTION A • UNIT 1: COMPUTER HARDWARE & FUNDAMENTALS [5 Questions • 5 Marks]
01. Which internal component is widely known as the 'Brain' of the computer because it interprets and executes program instructions and performs all calculations?
(A) Computer Monitor
(B) Hard Disk Drive (HDD)
(C) Central Processing Unit (CPU)
(D) Computer Keyboard
02. Which type of computer memory is 'volatile', meaning that all data stored in it is instantly erased when the computer is turned off or restarted?
(A) Read-Only Memory (ROM)
(B) Random Access Memory (RAM)
(C) Hard Disk Drive (HDD)
(D) USB Pen Drive
03. Which of the following devices is strictly an **Input Device** used to enter data, text, and commands into the computer?
(A) Computer Keyboard
(B) Display Monitor
(C) Laser Printer
(D) Audio Speaker
04. What type of modern storage drive uses electronic flash memory chips with zero spinning parts, providing significantly faster boot and file opening speeds than a mechanical Hard Disk?
(A) Compact Disc (CD-ROM)
(B) Floppy Disk
(C) Magnetic Cassette Tape
(D) Solid State Drive (SSD)
05. A computer network that interconnects computers and devices within a limited physical geographical area, such as a single computer lab, office building, or college campus, is known as a:
(A) Wide Area Network (WAN)
(B) Local Area Network (LAN)
(C) Metropolitan Area Network (MAN)
(D) Global Satellite Network
SECTION B • UNIT 2: OPERATING SYSTEMS & WINDOWS MANAGEMENT [5 Questions • 5 Marks]
06. What is the primary function of an **Operating System** (such as Windows 10, macOS, or Linux) on a personal computer?
(A) To act as a bridge between computer hardware and the user, managing software programs, files, and memory
(B) To design and edit marketing brochures
(C) To supply physical electrical power from the wall socket
(D) To manufacture microprocessor chips
07. Which standard keyboard shortcut is used in Windows and Microsoft Office to **Copy** selected text, images, or files to the clipboard?
(A) Ctrl + X
(B) Ctrl + V
(C) Ctrl + C
(D) Ctrl + Z
08. When you delete a document or folder from your hard drive by pressing the `Delete` key in Windows, where does it temporarily go so you can easily restore it if needed?
(A) Device Manager
(B) Control Panel
(C) Windows Update
(D) Recycle Bin
09. Which built-in Windows maintenance tool safely scans your hard drive to find and delete temporary files, system cache, and empty the Recycle Bin to free up disk storage?
(A) Disk Cleanup (or Storage Sense)
(B) Paint 3D
(C) Windows Media Player
(D) Notepad
10. When stepping away from your workstation or computer desk in an office or college lab, which keyboard shortcut instantly **Locks** your Windows screen to prevent unauthorized access?
(A) Alt + F4
(B) Windows Key + L
(C) Ctrl + Shift + Esc
(D) Windows Key + P

(A) Plus sign (`+`)
(B) Hashtag (`#`)
(C) Dollar sign (`$`)
(D) Equal sign (`=`)

(A) `=ADD(A1:A10)`
(B) `=SUM(A1:A10)`
(C) `=TOTAL(A1:A10)`
(D) `=COUNT(A1:A10)`

(A) Slide
(B) Worksheet
(C) Document
(D) Artboard

(A) F1

(C) F5
(D) F12
SECTION D • UNIT 4: INTERNET, CLOUD & DIGITAL SERVICES [5 Questions • 5 Marks]
16. In a complete website web address such as `https://www.google.com`, what does the common prefix acronym **WWW** stand for?
(A) Wide World Web
(B) Western Wireless Web
(C) World Web Wireless
(D) World Wide Web
17. Which of the following is a **Web Browser** application used to open, view, and navigate pages and websites across the internet?
(A) Google Chrome
(B) Microsoft Excel
(C) Adobe Photoshop
(D) Windows Media Player
18. When sending an official email to multiple recipients, which address field should you use to hide recipient email addresses from each other to preserve their privacy?
(A) To
(B) CC (Carbon Copy)
(C) BCC (Blind Carbon Copy)
(D) Subject Line
19. Which widely used cloud storage service provided by Google gives users 15 GB of free online storage to save files, backup photos, and share documents from anywhere?
(A) Google Maps
(B) Google Drive
(C) Google Play Store
(D) Google Translate
20. When using UPI mobile payment apps (such as Google Pay, PhonePe, or Paytm), which is the most critical security rule regarding your secret **UPI PIN**?
(A) You must enter your UPI PIN whenever someone wants to send or transfer money into your bank account.
(B) You should share your secret UPI PIN with customer support executives over the phone.
(C) You must enter your UPI PIN to claim lottery or refund prizes from unknown callers.
(D) You NEVER enter your UPI PIN to receive money; entering your UPI PIN is only required when you are transferring (paying) money OUT of your account.

(B) Hardware
(C) Freeware
(D) Firmware

(B) To automatically repair damaged hardware cables and monitor screens
(C) To detect, prevent, quarantine, and remove malicious viruses and security threats from your computer

(C) Screen Casting
